Assumptions and limits
  • Cruise 36 km/h (10 m/s), an assumption. Routes follow the dataset's real node connections; line lengths come from the dataset.

  • On a de-energized line the unit hover-checks only: no perch, no charge.
  • Line geometry is simplified to six vertices per line for the browser. Telemetry values are simulated, not measured.
  • Not for live use. Flying near energized lines needs regulatory approval and trained crews.
