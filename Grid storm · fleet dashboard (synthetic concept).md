Assumptions and limits

- All five start charged at hub SUBSTATION_07 at T+00:00.
- Endurance 30 min per sortie; each sortie starts at full endurance.
- Transit at an assumed 20 km/h. Times are set to fit that speed; the map draws straight-line transits.
- Recharge takes 3 h on an energized line. Only LINE_024 is known de-energized; every other line is assumed live, not verified.
- On a de-energized line a drone can hover-check only: no perch, no charge.
- All sensor values are HYPOTHETICAL demo readings, not measurements.
- Not for live use. Flying near energized lines needs regulatory approval and trained crews.
